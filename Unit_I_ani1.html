1. Ohm's Law

V = IR

For a resistive element under constant physical conditions, current is directly proportional to voltage. The fundamental relationship is V = IR.

V = I R    |    I = V/R    |    R = V/I

Unit: V in volts, I in amperes and R in ohms (Ω).

Numerical Problem

A 24 V DC supply is connected to a 8 Ω resistor. Find the current through the resistor and power absorbed.

2. Kirchhoff's Laws

KCL + KVL

Kirchhoff's Current Law (KCL)

At any node, the algebraic sum of currents is zero. Total current entering a node equals total current leaving it.

ΣIin = ΣIout

Kirchhoff's Voltage Law (KVL)

Around any closed loop, the algebraic sum of voltage rises and drops is zero.

ΣV = 0

Numerical Problem

At a node, 8 A and 3 A enter the node. If 5 A leaves through one branch, find the current leaving through the remaining branch.

3. Series and Parallel Circuits

Series Circuit

In series, the same current flows through every resistor.

Req = R1 + R2 + ... + Rn

Parallel Circuit

In parallel, the voltage across each branch is the same.

1/Req = 1/R1 + 1/R2 + ... + 1/Rn

Numerical Problem

Two resistors of 10 Ω and 15 Ω are connected in parallel across a 30 V source. Find equivalent resistance and total current.

4. Ideal and Practical Voltage and Current Sources

Ideal Voltage Source

Maintains constant terminal voltage independent of load current. Internal resistance is zero.

Practical Voltage Source

Modelled as an ideal voltage source in series with internal resistance.

Ideal Current Source

Maintains constant current independent of terminal voltage. Internal resistance is infinite.

Practical Current Source

Modelled as an ideal current source in parallel with a large internal resistance.

Numerical Problem

A 12 V practical voltage source has an internal resistance of 1 Ω and supplies a 5 Ω load. Find load current and terminal voltage.

5. Mesh and Nodal Analysis

Mesh Analysis

Mesh analysis applies KVL to independent loops. Assign mesh currents and solve simultaneous equations.

Nodal Analysis

Nodal analysis applies KCL at essential nodes. Select a reference node and determine node voltages.

Numerical Problem — Nodal Analysis

A node V is connected to a 10 V source through 2 Ω and to ground through 3 Ω. Find V.

6. Source Transformation

A voltage source V in series with R can be transformed into an equivalent current source I in parallel with the same R.

I = V/R     R remains unchanged

Numerical Problem

Transform a 20 V source in series with 5 Ω into an equivalent current source. Find source current and parallel resistance.
