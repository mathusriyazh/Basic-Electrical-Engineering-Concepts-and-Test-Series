OHM'S LAW – NUMERICAL TEST

Power, Voltage, Current and Resistance

20 Questions × 2 Marks = 40 Marks

Instructions
  • Each question carries 2 marks.
  • Each circuit contains at least four resistors.
  • Enter only the numerical answer.
  • Use Ω, V, A or W according to the question.
  • Decimal answers are accepted with a small tolerance.
Important Formulae
V = IR
I = V/R
R = V/I
P = VI
P = I²R
P = V²/R
Series: RT = R1 + R2 + ...
Parallel: 1/RT = 1/R1 + 1/R2 + ...
Q1. [2 Marks]
Four resistors of 4 Ω, 6 Ω, 10 Ω and R Ω are connected in series across a 60 V battery. The voltage across the 6 Ω resistor is 12 V. Determine the value of the unknown resistor R.
Ω
Solution:
Voltage across 6 Ω = 12 V
I = V/R = 12/6 = 2 A
Total resistance = 60/2 = 30 Ω
30 = 4 + 6 + 10 + R
R = 10 Ω
Q2. [2 Marks]
Four resistors of 5 Ω, 10 Ω, 15 Ω and R Ω are connected in series. The current through the circuit is 2 A. The power dissipated by the unknown resistor is 80 W. Determine R.
Ω
Solution:
P = I²R
80 = 2²R
80 = 4R
R = 20 Ω
Q3. [2 Marks]
Four resistors of 6 Ω, 9 Ω, 12 Ω and R Ω are connected in parallel. The current through the 6 Ω branch is 3 A and the total current supplied by the source is 8.5 A. Determine the value of R.
Ω
Solution:
Voltage across the parallel network:
V = IR = 3 × 6 = 18 V

Current through 9 Ω = 18/9 = 2 A
Current through 12 Ω = 18/12 = 1.5 A

Current through R:
IR = 8.5 − 3 − 2 − 1.5 = 2 A

R = V/I = 18/2
R = 9 Ω
Q4. [2 Marks]
Four resistors of 4 Ω, 6 Ω, 10 Ω and R Ω are connected in series. The circuit carries a current of 2 A and the total power supplied by the battery is 200 W. Determine the battery voltage and the unknown resistance R.
Ω
Solution:
P = VI
200 = V × 2
V = 100 V

RT = V/I = 100/2 = 50 Ω
50 = 4 + 6 + 10 + R
R = 30 Ω

Battery voltage = 100 V
Q5. [2 Marks]
A 24 V supply is connected across four parallel resistors of 6 Ω, 12 Ω, 8 Ω and R Ω. The current through the 8 Ω branch is 3 A. Determine the value of R if the total current supplied is 10 A.
Ω

Q7. [2 Marks]
Four resistors of 4 Ω, 8 Ω, 12 Ω and R Ω are connected in parallel. The current through the 12 Ω resistor is 2 A. The total current is 8 A. Determine R.
Ω
Solution:
V = IR = 2 × 12 = 24 V

I through 4 Ω = 24/4 = 6 A
I through 8 Ω = 24/8 = 3 A
I through 12 Ω = 2 A

These known branches already draw:
6 + 3 + 2 = 11 A

Since this exceeds the stated total current of 8 A, the data are physically inconsistent. No positive resistor value exists for R.
Q8. [2 Marks]
A circuit consists of a 4 Ω resistor in series with three parallel resistors of 6 Ω, 12 Ω and R Ω. The supply voltage is 24 V and the total current is 3 A. Determine R.
Ω
Solution:
RT = V/I = 24/3 = 8 Ω
Parallel equivalent:
RP = 8 − 4 = 4 Ω

1/4 = 1/6 + 1/12 + 1/R
1/R = 1/4 − 1/6 − 1/12
1/R = 0

This corresponds to an open circuit. R → ∞ Ω

Q13. [2 Marks]
A 30 V battery supplies a circuit containing a 5 Ω resistor in series with a parallel combination of 10 Ω, 15 Ω and R Ω. The current through the 5 Ω resistor is 2 A. Determine R.
Ω
Solution:
Total current = 2 A
Voltage across 5 Ω:
V = IR = 2 × 5 = 10 V

Voltage across parallel network:
VP = 30 − 10 = 20 V

Current through 10 Ω = 20/10 = 2 A
Current through 15 Ω = 20/15 = 1.333 A

The total current entering the parallel network is 2 A, which is already exceeded by the 10 Ω branch alone. The given data are inconsistent.
Q14. [2 Marks]
Four resistors of 4 Ω, 8 Ω, 12 Ω and R Ω are connected in parallel. The power consumed by the 8 Ω resistor is 32 W. The total current supplied by the battery is 12 A. Determine R.
Ω
Solution:
P = V²/R
32 = V²/8
V² = 256
V = 16 V

Current through 4 Ω = 16/4 = 4 A
Current through 8 Ω = 2 A
Current through 12 Ω = 16/12 = 1.333 A

IR = 12 − 4 − 2 − 1.333
IR = 4.667 A

R = V/I
R ≈ 16/4.667
R ≈ 3.43 Ω

Q18. [2 Marks]

Q20. [2 Marks]
A circuit contains a 4 Ω resistor in series with a parallel combination of 8 Ω, 12 Ω and R Ω. The total circuit power is 96 W and the supply voltage is 24 V. Determine the value of R.
Ω
Solution:
Total current:
I = P/V = 96/24 = 4 A

Total resistance:
RT = V/I = 24/4 = 6 Ω
Parallel equivalent:
RP = 6 − 4 = 2 Ω

1/2 = 1/8 + 1/12 + 1/R
1/R = 1/2 − 1/8 − 1/12
1/R = 12/24 − 3/24 − 2/24
1/R = 7/24
R = 24/7 ≈ 3.43 Ω
