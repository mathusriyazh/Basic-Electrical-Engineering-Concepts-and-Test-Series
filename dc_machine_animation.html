DC Machine

Function

A DC machine converts mechanical energy into electrical energy or electrical energy into mechanical energy.

Construction

The machine consists of a stationary part called the stator and a rotating part called the armature or rotor.

Important Formula

Eg = (P Φ Z N) / (60 A)
