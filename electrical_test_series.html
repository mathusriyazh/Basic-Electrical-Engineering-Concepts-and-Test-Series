Question 21 (Mixed Rating Lamps Circuit Design) 10 Marks
A circuit consists of two lamps L1 (100 V, 60 W) and L2 (100 V, 100 W) connected in series across a 200 V DC mains as shown in schematic.
To operate both lamps safely at rated power, a resistor RS is connected in parallel with L1.

(a) Calculate normal rated current I1 of Lamp L1 and I2 of Lamp L2 in Amperes. [3 Marks]
(b) Determine the value of shunt resistance RS in Ohms across L1. [4 Marks]
(c) Calculate total power supplied by the 200 V supply in Watts. [3 Marks]

Question 23 (3-Lamp Series-Parallel Network Optimization) 10 Marks
Three lamps L1 (100 V, 40 W), L2 (100 V, 60 W), and L3 (100 V, 100 W) are configured such that L1 and L2 are in parallel, and this combination is connected in series with L3 across a 200 V DC supply.
A single external resistor RX is added in parallel with the (L1 || L2) group so that ALL THREE lamps operate at their rated voltages.

(a) Calculate individual rated currents I1, I2, and I3 in Amperes. [3 Marks]
(b) Determine required current through resistor RX and compute its resistance in Ohms. [4 Marks]
(c) Calculate total power drawn from supply and percentage power lost in RX. [3 Marks]
