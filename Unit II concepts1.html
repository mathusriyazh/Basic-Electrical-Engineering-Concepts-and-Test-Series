Complex Impedance & Vector Equations

Practice Numerical Problem: Series R-L-C Circuit

Given Parameters:

  • Resistance ($R$) = $10\text{ }\Omega$
  • Inductance ($L$) = $31.8\text{ mH} = 0.0318\text{ H}$
  • Capacitance ($C$) = $63.7\text{ }\mu\text{F} = 63.7 \times 10^{-6}\text{ F}$
  • Supply Voltage ($V_{rms}$) = $230\text{ V}$, Frequency ($f$) = $50\text{ Hz}$

Formula Roadmap to Solve This Problem:

  1. Inductive Reactance: $X_L = 2 \pi f L = 2 \times \pi \times 50 \times 0.0318$
  2. Capacitive Reactance: $X_C = \frac{1}{2 \pi f C} = \frac{1}{2 \times \pi \times 50 \times 63.7 \times 10^{-6}}$
  3. Total Impedance Magnitude: $|Z| = \sqrt{R^2 + (X_L - X_C)^2}$
  4. Power Factor: $\text{PF} = \cos\phi = \frac{R}{|Z|}$ (State if leading or lagging based on whether $X_C > X_L$ or $X_L > X_C$)

Enter your calculated values below to test your solution:

Step-by-Step Solution:

Step 1: Calculate $X_L$
$$X_L = 2 \times \pi \times 50 \times 0.0318 \approx 10.0\text{ }\Omega$$

Step 2: Calculate $X_C$
$$X_C = \frac{1}{2 \times \pi \times 50 \times 63.7 \times 10^{-6}} = \frac{1}{0.02001} \approx 50.0\text{ }\Omega$$

Step 3: Calculate Total Impedance $|Z|$
$$|Z| = \sqrt{R^2 + (X_L - X_C)^2} = \sqrt{10^2 + (10 - 50)^2} = \sqrt{100 + 1600} = \sqrt{1700} \approx 41.23\text{ }\Omega$$

Step 4: Calculate Power Factor
$$\text{PF} = \frac{R}{|Z|} = \frac{10}{41.23} \approx 0.242\text{ (Leading, because } X_C > X_L \text{)}$$

4. Parallel AC Circuits & Admittance

In parallel AC circuits, total current is solved using Admittance ($\mathbf{Y} = G + jB = 1/\mathbf{Z}$).

$$\mathbf{I}_T = \mathbf{I}_1 + \mathbf{I}_2 = \mathbf{V} \mathbf{Y}_T = \mathbf{V} (G_T + jB_T)$$

5. Power & Power Factor in AC Circuits

AC circuits deliver Active Power ($P$), Reactive Power ($Q$), and Apparent Power ($S$). The Power Factor is $\cos\phi = P/S$.

Power Equations

Active Power ($P$): $P = V I \cos\phi \quad (\text{Watts, W})$

Reactive Power ($Q$): $Q = V I \sin\phi \quad (\text{VAR})$

Apparent Power ($S$): $S = V I = \sqrt{P^2 + Q^2} \quad (\text{VA})$

Complex Power ($\mathbf{S}$): $\mathbf{S} = \mathbf{V} \mathbf{I}^* = P + jQ$

Numerical Problem

A single-phase motor absorbs $5\text{ kW}$ at a lagging power factor of $0.8$ from a $230\text{ V}, 50\text{ Hz}$ supply. Determine the reactive power $Q$ and apparent power $S$.
