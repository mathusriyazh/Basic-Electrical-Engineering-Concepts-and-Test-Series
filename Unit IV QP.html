Q1. THEORY 5 Marks
Explain the basic principles of domestic wiring and list the important wiring accessories used in a domestic electrical installation.
Answer / Solution

Domestic wiring is the arrangement of conductors, protective devices, switches and accessories used for distributing electrical energy safely within a building.

  • Conductors should be of suitable current-carrying capacity.
  • Switches should normally be connected in the phase conductor.
  • Suitable protective devices should be provided.
  • Equipment bodies should be properly earthed.
  • Wiring should be properly insulated and mechanically protected.

Common accessories include switches, socket outlets, plugs, fuses, MCBs, RCCBs, distribution boards and energy meters.

Q2. THEORY 5 Marks
Explain the purpose of earthing. State the important safety requirements associated with an electrical installation.
Answer / Solution
  • Earthing provides a low-resistance path for fault current.
  • It reduces the risk of electric shock.
  • It allows protective devices to operate during faults.
  • Exposed conductive parts should be connected to protective earth.
  • Earthing conductors should be properly sized and mechanically protected.
  • Earthing connections should be inspected and maintained periodically.
Q3. THEORY 5 Marks
Draw and explain the single-line diagram of an electrical power supply system from generating station to consumers.
Answer / Solution
Generating Station → Step-up Transformer → Transmission → Receiving Station → Substation → Distribution → Consumer

The generating station produces electrical power. A step-up transformer raises the voltage for transmission. High-voltage transmission carries power over long distances. At the receiving station and substations, the voltage is progressively reduced. Distribution systems then supply consumers.

Q10. THEORY 5 Marks
Explain power rating and star rating of electrical appliances. How do these ratings help consumers?
Answer / Solution

Power rating indicates the electrical power normally consumed by an appliance under its rated operating condition.

Star rating is an energy-efficiency indication used for applicable electrical appliances. A higher efficiency rating generally indicates lower energy consumption for the same service, subject to the product and rating conditions.

Q2. THEORY 5 Marks
Explain plate earthing and state its applications.
Answer / Solution

In plate earthing, a metal plate electrode is buried in the ground and connected to the installation through an earthing conductor. The electrode provides a low-resistance path for fault current.

Q3. THEORY 5 Marks
Explain why high voltage is used for electrical power transmission.
Answer / Solution
P = VI
Ploss = I²R

For a given transmitted power, increasing voltage reduces current. Since conductor loss is proportional to I², reducing current reduces transmission losses.

Q8. THEORY 5 Marks
Explain the terms line voltage, phase voltage, line current and phase current in a three-phase system.
Answer / Solution

  • Phase voltage is the voltage across an individual phase of the load.
  • Line current is the current in a line conductor.
  • Phase current is the current through an individual phase impedance.

Q6. THEORY 5 Marks
Explain star and delta connections of a three-phase balanced load.
Answer / Solution

In a star connection, one end of each phase impedance is connected to a common point. In a delta connection, the three phase impedances are connected end-to-end to form a closed loop.

Star: VL=√3Vph, IL=Iph
Delta: VL=Vph, IL=√3Iph

Q2. THEORY 5 Marks
What is earthing? Explain how earthing improves electrical safety.
Answer / Solution

Earthing connects exposed conductive parts of electrical equipment to earth through a low-resistance path. During an insulation fault, the fault current can flow through the protective path, helping protective devices disconnect the faulty circuit and reducing shock risk.
