Important Formulae

Inductive Reactance: XL = 2πfL
Capacitive Reactance: XC = 1/(2πfC)
Impedance of Series R-L: Z = R + jXL
Impedance of Series R-C: Z = R - jXC
Impedance of Series R-L-C: Z = R + j(XL - XC)
Magnitude: |Z| = √(R² + X²)
Ohm's Law for AC: I = V/Z
Real Power: P = VI cosφ
Reactive Power: Q = VI sinφ
Apparent Power: S = VI
Power Factor: cosφ = P/S
