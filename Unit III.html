⚡ Energy Conversion Systems

This unit introduces the fundamental principles and applications of electrical energy conversion systems. The learning material combines theory, animated concepts, block diagrams, formulas, numerical problems and interactive simulations.

Learning sequence: Magnetic Principles → DC Machines → Transformer → Wind Energy → Solar PV → Inverter → UPS

02. DC Generator
Construction, working principle, EMF generation and applications
03. DC Motor
Back EMF, torque production, speed control and applications
04. Transformer
Construction, working, EMF equation, losses and efficiency
05. Wind Energy
Wind turbine, generator, converter and grid connection
06. Solar PV
Grid-connected and standalone photovoltaic systems
07. Inverter
DC-to-AC conversion, switching, PWM and filtering
08. UPS
Offline, line-interactive and online UPS systems
